Delete your account

Use this form to request permanent deletion of your MERQ POS account and store data, without needing to open the app.

This cannot be undone

Deleting your account permanently removes your store, products, staff accounts, and complete transaction history. If other people are members of your store, only your own access is removed and the store keeps running for them.

What gets deleted:

  • Your store profile and settings
  • All products, categories and ingredients
  • All staff accounts and PINs
  • Your complete sales and transaction history
  • Payment methods and receipt settings

Submitting this form opens an email to merq@prohubapps.com with your request. We process deletion requests within 7 days and will confirm by email once your account and data have been removed. If your email client doesn't open automatically, email us directly at merq@prohubapps.com with the same details.
